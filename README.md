A 100-word monologue for Karghul, Lord of the Ashen Spire — a fantasy
villain forty-three years into an empire built on grain levies and
tolls on the Dunhollow road, addressing the hero who climbed his nine
hundred steps. His tiredness lives in the pauses, the trailing
thoughts and the small deflections, never in a plain statement — and
his grand final scheme turns out to be handing the hero the crown,
because the crown comes with the paperwork.

## How it runs

`main.py` writes the monologue to `/output/entry.txt`. The text is
fixed and deterministic: the same bytes on every run (101 words,
542 characters including the trailing newline, inside the gate of
1–600 characters).

```sh
python3 main.py
```
